Every site, optimized

Your settings, moved to each of the simulator's sites. At every site the optimizer sizes the solar, battery, gas or nuclear units and grid connection for a grid-connected, a hybrid and an islanded design, each for the highest NPV that meets the uptime target, and keeps the best. Site-driven inputs (solar yield, grid price and CO₂, carbon price, weather) follow the site; everything else is yours.
