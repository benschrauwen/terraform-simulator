Solar and battery price sweeps

At each price the best design across a grid-connected, a hybrid and an islanded architecture is optimized for every site, as on the sites page: the highest NPV that meets the uptime target without the backup diesel. The solar sweep moves installed solar (panel + BOS, their split kept) with the battery held at a price you choose; the battery sweep moves the installed battery ($/kWh of a 4-hour battery: energy + power, their split kept) with solar held. Each sweep shows CO₂e, NPV and excess electricity: on-site generation the site can neither use nor store, curtailed or (when export is on) sold to the grid.
