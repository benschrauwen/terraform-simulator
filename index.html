US policy caveats
Terraform's whitepaper calls the green-electricity production credit `45E`. The relevant Internal Revenue Code sections are `§45` (for projects that began construction before 2025) and its successors `§45Y` (production credit) and `§48E` (investment credit), which this app models; IRC `§45E` is an unrelated small-employer pension credit. Credit amounts and deadlines are as of Oct 2026 (see each scheme's note).
This panel still models one named program or framework at a time unless you choose `Custom mixed support`. In real US projects, solar-side and product-side incentives can interact through ownership, transferability, and tax structure, so treat the result as a TEA simplification rather than legal advice.

🌱 Environmental Impact ▾

Captured CO₂ is either bound in products or vented. Fuel carbon returns to the air when burned, so capture is not removal: net avoided counts only the fossil combustion CO₂ that the methane, methanol and MTG fuels displace (methane at 0.0528 t CO₂/MCF, pure CH₄ at 19.25 kg/MCF), excluding upstream emissions and methane leakage. Nothing is stored permanently.
