Solar resource and PV

  • PVGIS v5.3 PVcalc API and PVGIS online tool (European Commission JRC) — annual yield and irradiation for every Earth site preset (optimal fixed tilt, 14% system loss).
  • Global Solar Atlas (Solargis v2.2) — diffuse fraction per preset and PVOUT cross-checks.
  • Annual Technology Baseline 2025, utility-scale PV (National Laboratory of the Rockies, formerly NREL) — cost context.
  • Jurchen PEG FAQ and PEG design — high-density East-West mounting.
  • J. W. Spencer (1971), "Fourier series representation of the position of the sun", Search 2(5):172 — solar declination and equation of time.
  • F. Kasten and A. T. Young (1989), "Revised optical air mass tables and approximation formula", Applied Optics 28:4735 — air mass.
  • A. B. Meinel and M. P. Meinel (1976), Applied Solar Energy: An Introduction, Addison-Wesley — clear-sky direct-beam model.
  • J. E. Hay and J. A. Davies (1980), "Calculation of the solar radiation incident on an inclined surface", Proc. First Canadian Solar Radiation Data Workshop — diffuse transposition onto tilted and tracking planes.

Chemistry and process routes

  • V. Vogl, M. Åhman and L. J. Nilsson (2018), "Assessment of hydrogen direct reduction for fossil-free steelmaking", Journal of Cleaner Production 203:736–745 — H₂-DRI + EAF energy split.
  • International Aluminium Institute, primary aluminium smelting power consumption — Hall-Héroult electricity intensity.
  • NIST Chemistry WebBook — standard enthalpies behind the reaction-energy floors (CO₂ → C, CaCO₃ → CaO, SiO₂ → Si, CO₂ → CO) and the stoichiometric constants.
  • US EPA greenhouse gas equivalencies: calculations and references — pipeline-gas CO₂ per MCF, compared with the pure-CH₄ factor the app uses.
  • NOAA GML Mauna Loa annual mean CO₂ — atmospheric CO₂ concentration.
  • AACE International Recommended Practice 18R-97, Cost Estimate Classification System — Class 4 (feasibility) accuracy range of −15…−30% / +20…+50%; the wide end sets the NPV tornado's process CAPEX bounds.
